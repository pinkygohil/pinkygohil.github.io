How I lead

Build the system, teach it, then step back.

Across my work, I've found that good leadership isn't about being involved in every decision. It's about giving people the context, tools, and confidence to make those decisions themselves.

  1. 01

    Teach the why, trust the how.

    I want my team to understand the thinking behind decisions, so they can make good ones on their own.

  2. 02

    Give ownership, make room to grow.

    I believe good work happens when people feel trusted to make decisions, take ownership, and learn from each other.

  3. 03

    Value thinking, not just experience.

    I value how people approach problems and work through unfamiliar challenges just as much as what they already know.

  4. 04

    Stay curious, be willing to change.

    I try not to get too attached to my first idea. If the evidence points in a different direction, I'm willing to rethink the approach.
