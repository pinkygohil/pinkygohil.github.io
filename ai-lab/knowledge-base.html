Case study · Working with Claude

One library for every case study, and on-brand slides in minutes

At NeenOpal, managers came to my design team every time they needed a pitch, and each one took about a day. I built an internal knowledge base with Claude: one searchable library for case studies, use cases, playbooks, and content that turns any case study into an on-brand slide. This page shows how it came together, using a demo with invented data.

Internal tool · NeenOpal Demo data · Fernwood Data Co. (fictional) Exports editable PowerPoint Slack-linked content pipeline
Knowledge base session
01 The problem
PG
Pinky

Our case studies, use cases, playbooks, and decks are spread across drives, the website, and Slack. Every time a manager needs a pitch, they come to my design team, and it takes about a day. Can we build one place that holds all of it and turns any case study into an on-brand slide?

C
Claude

Yes. Before I build anything, three questions: who searches it, how do they search, and what do they need to walk away with?

02 Who it's for
PG
Pinky

Sales and delivery managers across the company. They look things up by industry or service, and they need a slide they can drop straight into a pitch without asking design.

C
Claude

Then the library should be organized around how they ask, not how the files are stored:

LayerWhat goes in it
CollectionsCase studies, use cases, playbooks, capability decks, brochures, videos, and blogs
FiltersIndustry, practice area, work type, and AI approach
SearchAcross titles, clients, descriptions, and the tech used
03 Brand rules first
PG
Pinky

The slides have to follow our design style exactly. If they don't, managers will keep coming back to us to fix them.

C
Claude

Then the brand system goes in before any layout: palette, type, logo rules, and spacing. I'll build two layouts, Standard and Detailed, from the same fields, so every slide comes out the same way.

Every slide is generated from the same structured fields, so there is nothing left for a manager to restyle.
04 Fixing the first draft
PG
Pinky

Long titles overflow the slide, and some case studies don't have three results to show.

C
Claude

I'll size the text to each box by measuring how many lines it needs, so long titles shrink instead of spilling over. Where a case study has fewer than three results, the empty slots show context, such as the industry or platform.

No invented numbers. Slides only show approved figures, each with a source.
05 Slides people can edit
PG
Pinky

Managers will want to tweak the wording before a meeting.

C
Claude

The export builds real PowerPoint text and shapes, not pictures of slides, so everything stays editable. Managers can download one slide or a whole selection as a single deck.

06 Clients who can't be named
PG
Pinky

Some clients can't be named in a pitch.

C
Claude

I've added a switch that replaces client names with a short description, like "A regional property insurer," on every card and slide.

07 Keeping it current
PG
Pinky

A library goes stale unless new content keeps flowing in. Right now requests come in over Slack and get lost. It should also check our website, LinkedIn, and YouTube so we know what's already out there.

C
Claude

Then the library needs a pipeline in front of it:

  • A request with a short brief
  • An AI draft in house style
  • Review and approval
  • Publish to the website, where it lands in the library with a ready slide

Every step posts to a Slack channel, so nothing gets lost in a thread.

08 Videos from the same material
PG
Pinky

Can it also make a short demo video from the same material?

C
Claude

Yes. Any request can be flagged for a demo video, which is built from the approved content using the video template, so it matches the slides.

Pinky

Key takeaways

What it changed

The knowledge base is used across NeenOpal. It took the design team out of routine pitch requests, without lowering the standard of what goes out.

Pitches in minutes, not a day

A pitch that used to take about a day now takes minutes. Managers find the right case study and export the slide themselves.

No back and forth with design

Every slide follows NeenOpal's design style, so managers no longer send pitches back to my team to fix.

One place for everything

Case studies, use cases, playbooks, and content across industries and services now live in one library anyone in the company can search.

Videos from the same material

The same content can become a short demo video, so slides and videos always tell the same story.
